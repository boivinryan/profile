ART PORTFOLIO

Selected Works · 2005–2026

RYAN BOIVIN

The Artist

This portfolio presents a selection of artworks by Ryan Boivin, spanning figurative portraiture, academic drawing, and contemporary expressionist painting.

The collection explores the human face, urban environments, architectural forms, colour, and atmosphere through a variety of artistic mediums.

From detailed graphite portrait studies to expressive cityscapes, these works document an evolving interest in representation, observation, and visual expression.
